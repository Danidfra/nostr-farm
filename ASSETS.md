## What the artwork is

The game loads its farm background and crop images at run time from the
`cozy-pixel-v1` renderpack in
[Danidfra/farm-nostr-game](https://github.com/Danidfra/farm-nostr-game),
pinned to commit `26bf77115a46fea907bc0a5e1c135a3501a59be4`
(`src/world/renderpack/registry.ts`):

- `assets/backgrounds/farm.png`
- `assets/crops/carrot.png`, `parsnip.png`, `pumpkin.png`, `strawberry.png`,
  `crop-rotten.png`

These images were generated with ChatGPT image generation (OpenAI) by the
project author. They were not drawn by hand. They do not come from an asset
pack and were not made by another artist.

The app icons in `public/` are part of this repository (added in `fcdf1e9`
with a hand-written SVG) and fall under the code's MIT license.
